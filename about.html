Welcome to Ektham Hotels.

Welcome to Ektham Hotels, where comfort meets exceptional hospitality in the spiritual city of Haridwar. Strategically located on the Main Road near Shantikunj, our hotel offers the perfect destination for pilgrims, tourists, business travellers, and families.

Whether you're visiting Haridwar for a spiritual journey, leisure, business, or a family celebration, Ektham Hotels provides a perfect blend of modern comfort, warm hospitality, and outstanding service. Our thoughtfully designed property features 36 premium rooms (36 Keys), an in-house Family Restaurant, dedicated spaces for birthday parties and kitty parties, and a huge secure parking area, ensuring a comfortable and memorable stay for every guest.

Your Gateway to Peace in the Heart of Haridwar.

Ektham Hotels is a fast-growing hospitality brand committed to delivering memorable guest experiences through exceptional service, comfortable accommodations, quality dining, and modern facilities.

Built on the principles of trust, integrity, comfort, and personalized hospitality, our hotel caters to pilgrims, leisure travellers, business professionals, and families visiting Haridwar. With experienced leadership and a passion for excellence, Ektham Hotels continues to set new benchmarks in hospitality while creating memorable experiences for every guest.
