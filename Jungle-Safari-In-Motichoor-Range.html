Into the Green: Experience the Thrill of the Wild, Right Next Door.

Located in the foothills of the Himalayas, between the towns of Motichur Range, Haridwar, Rajaji National Park is a pristine wildlife sanctuary offering an extraordinary experience for nature lovers, wildlife enthusiasts, and adventure seekers. Covering over 1,000 square kilometers, the park is home to a rich diversity of flora and fauna, including tigers, elephants, leopards, and more than 300 species of birds. A safari through Rajaji National Park is a thrilling journey into the heart of the wild.

Step into the Wild

Safari Timings

Conducted in two slots daily —morning (typically 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM) and evening (3:00 PM to 6:00 PM), with seasonal time shifts during peak summer.

Duration & Format: Standard jeep (Gypsy) safaris last about 2 to 3 hours, accommodating up to 6 persons per vehicle with a mandatory authorized guide.

Fees & Permits: Entry permits are required per person (₹150 for Indian nationals and ₹600 for foreign visitors), alongside vehicle entry charges and gypsy rental fees. Advance online or gate booking is strongly recommended due to high demand.

Flora & Fauna: Characterized by lush broadleaf deciduous forests, grasslands, and water streams, making it a fantastic spot for spotting pachyderms, large herds of deer, wild boars, and numerous species of resident and migratory birds.

"Motichur Gate, Rajaji Tiger Reserve"

The Motichur Range of the Rajaji Tiger Reserve offers an immersive wildlife experience characterized by dense mixed-broadleaf forests, dry riverbeds (raos), and rolling hills. It serves as a vital wildlife corridor and a thriving sanctuary for free-ranging elephants, leopards, spotted deer, barking deer, wild boars, and over 300 species of resident and migratory birds.

The Motichur Gate acts as the primary access point for safari vehicles entering the Motichur wilderness zone, setting off along scenic forest tracks and streams.

Safari Experience: Features a winding 25-kilometer safari route traversing rich Sal forests and water channels, ideal for landscape photography and wildlife viewing.

Operational Slots: Operates in two main shifts daily—morning (approx. 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM) and afternoon (approx. 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM)—subject to seasonal adjustments by the forest department..

Vehicle & Guide Rules: Entry is strictly restricted to registered open-top jeeps (Gypsies) accompanied by an authorized forest guide to ensure safety and adherence to conservation norms.

How to Reach the Motichur Range

By Air: The nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport in Dehradun, located roughly 25 to 30 kilometers away. Taxis and cabs are readily available from the airport to reach the Motichur forest entry gate.
