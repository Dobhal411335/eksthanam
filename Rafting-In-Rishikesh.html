Timings & Experience

Rafting Timings & Operational Season

Morning to Afternoon Slots: Typically runs between 7:00 AM and 3:00 PM.

Preferred Slots: Morning sessions (around 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM) are most popular due to calmer weather and optimal wind conditions.

Open Season: Late September / October through June 20th of every year.

Monsoon Closure: Completely closed from July to mid-September due to high water levels, unpredictable currents, and safety regulations.

Why Choose Our Rafting Experience?

Certified Expert Guides: Navigate legendary rapids like Marine Drive, Roller Coaster, and The Wall with seasoned professionals who prioritize your safety above all else.

Top-Tier Equipment: We provide high-grade self-bailing rafts, international-standard helmets, and buoyant life jackets to keep you secure and comfortable on the water.

Breathtaking Scenery: Float past pristine pine forests, ancient cliffside temples, and dramatic Himalayan canyons that can only be witnessed from the middle of the holy river. Tailored Adventures: From family-friendly scenic stretches to extreme multi-grade rapid runs, we curate trips that match your exact comfort and thrill level.

Popular Rafting Stretches

Brahmpuri to Rishikesh (Grade I & II): Perfect for families, beginners, and nature lovers seeking a gentle 9-kilometer float with a splash of excitement and cliff-jumping opportunities.

Shivpuri to Rishikesh (Grade III): The sweet spot for adventure seekers! Tackle famous rapids like Return to Sender, Roller Coaster, and Golf Course across a thrilling 16-kilometer run.

Marine Drive to Rishikesh (Grade III & IV): A 24-kilometer endurance test for true thrill-seekers, featuring continuous heavy rapids and roaring drops that will get your heart racing.
